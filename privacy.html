Sample policy for a concept project

Privacy, in plain language.

Short sentences. No surprises. Here's the shape our real policy takes.

On this page

  1. What we collect
  2. How we use it
  3. Sharing
  4. Retention
  5. Your rights
  6. Contact

What we collect

Your account details: name, work email, and business name. Nothing more to get started.

The appointments you sync: client names, phone numbers, and visit times. We only read what reminders need.

The replies your clients send: confirmations, reschedules, and waitlist answers.

How we use it

To send reminders, refill cancelled slots from your waitlist, and show your reports.

That's the whole list. We never sell your data, and we show no ads to anyone.

Sharing

We share data only with the services that deliver it: your calendar provider and message channels.

No data brokers. No advertisers. A full sub-processor list is available on request.

Retention

We keep your data while your account is active. Cancel, and we delete it within 30 days of your request.

Encrypted backups rotate out within 90 days. After that, it's gone everywhere.

Your rights

Export, correct, or delete your data any time. One message to support starts it.

Your clients can opt out of messages by replying STOP. We honor it immediately.

Contact

Questions about privacy? Write to privacy@slotwise.example and a human will reply.
